15 · LIBRARY & PDF

Four levels, one tap each, and the page is the page.

The library goes from the Rebbeim down to a single sicha in four taps, and every level looks like the one before it. A PDF opens edge to edge, with nothing in the way until you tap.

The levels

LEVEL 1 · LIBRARYThe Rebbeim, then the other shelvesA row of nine portraits, the Rebbe first, then the shelves by subject. The farbrengens and Chitas & Rambam each have a card of their own. Board 16.
LEVEL 2 · SHELFIts sets as sections, its sefarim as coversLikkutei Sichos, Toras Menachem, Igros Kodesh and the rest, each as a row of title pages that scrolls sideways. Board 17.
LEVEL 3 · SEFERVolumes as numbers, contents belowA grid of the volume numbers, and the open volume's contents grouped as the sefer is. Board 18.
LEVEL 4 · READText, scan or recordingThe reader (board 10), the PDF (board 19) or the transcript page (board 6a).

Shelves that are clean

At the top level today the catalog holds nine empty shelves (Chassidus, Halacha, History, Letters, Maamarim, Minhagim, Sichos, Siddur, Teshuros). The library page never shows a shelf with nothing on it. HebrewBooks, Otzros and Sefaria are sources, not shelves, so they move to a “Where it came from” filter in ⋯. Additions (indexes, commentaries) sit on their sefer's page, never in among the sefarim.

The deep options, in one sheet

Every level has one Show button. It opens a sheet with three choices:
· Order: by volume, year, parsha or chapter (whatever the sefer has)
· Only: with text, with a recording, with a scan, or with a translation
· Also show: other printings and additions
The choices stay in the address, so a link shares them, and a Reset sits in the same sheet.

What a row says

A row gives the name, then one line in plain words: the date or parsha, and small icons for text, scan and recording. There are no ids, file names or source paths; those live on the item's ⋯ › Details. Machine text keeps its amber dot.

The PDF view

The page, edge to edgeThe page fills the screen. Tapping shows the bars, which hide again after three seconds. Pinch zooms the page, not the site, and a double tap zooms to the column. Turned sideways, it shows two pages as a spread.
One bar at the bottomA page slider shows the page number as you drag, then Pages (thumbnails), Text (the words of this page, where the site has them) and Search.
Listen while you readThe site's player stays as a slim bar above the page bar, as it does now. Your place in every PDF is remembered.
Everything else in ⋯Download (only when the rights allow it), where it came from, the printing, a reading copy the machine straightened (labelled), report a page, and go to a page.

On a larger screen

The shelves move into a column on the side, and a shelf shows its covers in a grid (board 21). A sefer shows its contents beside its volumes. A PDF shows its thumbnails in a side column that can be closed, and the page keys are ← →, Home and End.

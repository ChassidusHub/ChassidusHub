6b · THE TRANSCRIPT PAGE

Listen, follow, tap what is wrong.

The page where a recording is heard and read is also where most fixes start. You can't miss the word being said. The player is one thumb's reach away. A wrong word is two taps from fixed, and the recording waits for you while you type.

How a word looks

העולםBeing said now: solid red, the strongest thing on screen
ואפילו כלAlready said: full ink
אומרים לךStill to come: grey, in this paragraph only
[מילה]Tapped: ringed, with the word bar open
[מילה?]Not sure: amber, in brackets
[מילה]Your fix, waiting for approval: green underline

The paragraph being heard is lifted onto a card, and the others sit flat on the page. Each paragraph shows its time (tap it to play from there) and whether it is checked or still the machine's. Every colour sits next to a word or shape that says the same thing, so no one has to tell colours apart.

The player

The map: one segment for each paragraph, green where it has been checked and amber where it is still the machine's, filled up to where you are. Tap a segment to jump to it. You can see at a glance how much is left to check.

The play button is 68px and sits in the middle, under the thumb. Seeking back goes by seconds, and seeking forward goes by paragraphs, because that is how people listen to a shiur. The speed control steps through 1×, 1.25×, 1.5× and 0.75×. The player stays at the foot of the page, and it keeps playing as you move from page to page, as it does now.

Following, without fighting you

The page keeps the spoken paragraph in the middle of the screen. As soon as you scroll yourself, it stops following and shows one red button: Back to the word being said. Pressing play, or that button, starts following again. The text never jumps while you are reading something else.

Tap a word, by level

Read level: a tap plays from that word, and nothing else happens.
Help level: a tap also opens the word bar, with Fix, Hear again and Not sure. Tapping a second word selects everything up to it. Pause on tap (on by default at this level) stops the audio on the word, so you can look before going on.

Fixing, with the recording waiting

Fix pauses the audio and opens a short sheet. It shows the three words before and after the selection, a large field already holding the words, and Hear these words again, which plays from a little before them and stops after. Saving puts the fix in the tray and plays on from two words earlier, so you hear the fix in place.

Two places, one job each

The transcript page (6a): listen, and fix what you notice as you go. A strip at the top counts the paragraphs nobody has checked, with Check next to it.
Check mode (6): one paragraph at a time, answered Right, Fix or Not sure, for when you sit down to check a whole recording. Both use the same tray.

On a larger screen

The text sits in one centred column, and the player is a full-width bar at the foot of the page. Space play or pause · ← → 5 seconds · Shift + ← next paragraph · F fix · U not sure · [ ] slower or faster · Esc close Everything else lives in ⋯: the parts of a farbrengen, the video, the JEM link, the sync check and the history.

22 · SUGGESTIONS & MERGES

Say what changed in one sentence. Show only that.

A suggestion should be understood in five seconds by anyone, without knowing how the catalog is built. So it begins with a sentence in plain words. It then shows the changed words with a few words around them, never the whole text side by side. Nothing that is not words a reader sees ever appears: no ids, field paths, kinds of record or JSON.

1 · A sentence firstThe title is written from the change itself: “2 words fixed in Likkutei Sichos 1, Bereishis”. The person's own note, if they wrote one, comes under it. “תיקון תמלול” ×5 becomes “Transcript of [the shiur]: 6 words fixed, 2 paragraphs checked”.
2 · Only the change, with a little around itA change shows at most four words on each side, with “…” for the rest. “Show the paragraph” opens the full paragraph in place, and “Against the scan” shows the printed line, only when there is one.
3 · Nothing that is not wordsGone from view: rh-… ids, /body/he/14.3, “alignment-span”, “segment”, file hashes and source paths. Timing that moved together with a word fix is not shown; it is part of the fix. All of these stay in ⋯ › Details for the few who need them.
4 · Many alike, said once500 changes of the same kind read as one line with three examples: “500 recordings: the link moved from the media proxy to Drive”. Anything that differs from the pattern is listed apart, at the top.
5 · One clear decisionApprove, or Send back with a line saying why. Keep or Remove on each change appears only when a suggestion has more than one. Comments fold into “2 comments” until opened.
6 · Combine is offered, not neededWhen one person has several open suggestions on the same place (as there are today: five “תיקון תמלול” on one recording), the list offers “Combine these 5 into one”. The tray keeps it from happening again.

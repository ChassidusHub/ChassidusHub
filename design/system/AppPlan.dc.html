47 · THE CHASSIDUSHUB APP (reader/)

One system, two places: the site holds everything, the app goes with you.

The app (formerly Sichos Kodesh) already does the right thing: it opens on this week, in the kvius year, plays farbrengens, opens their texts and keeps them for offline. It keeps its jobs and its five tabs (השבוע · התוועדויות · הספרייה · חיפוש · שלי). It takes the site's look and parts, so moving between the two feels like one place.

Shared from the siteThe tokens (one theme-colors.css made from tokens.css), the logo and the book icon, the type scales, the player with its paragraph map and the highlighted word, the cards and rows, the Aa reading sheet, and the states.
The app's ownDownloads and offline (a progress ring on each farbrengen, and a storage page), playing every chapter as one queue, its PDF screen with “invert PDF in dark mode”, and following a month across the years.
Fix and ask go to the siteAs the plan says (step 3): the ⋯ menu on a farbrengen or a page has “Suggest a fix” and “Report”, which open chassidushub.org at that place, so signing in, the Check sheet and the tray stay in one place. “Ask the library” opens the site's Ask with the question.

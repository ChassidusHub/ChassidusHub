3 · EDITING & CHECKING

One way to fix anything: look, answer, move on.

Today there are four editors, each with its own buttons and its own words: one for editing a page, one for checking a scan, one for checking a transcript, and one for reviewing fixes. The new design keeps every one of their powers but has one shape, one set of answers and one way to send.

1 · The source the scan's line, the printed page, or the recording at this paragraph
2 · The words what the site has now, large. Tap a word to play from it or to select it.
3 · Three answers

The Check sheet

1The source sits on top and stays in view. It is the scan cut to the line being checked, the printed page next to a page's text, or the recording at this paragraph with Hear again and a speed control. For plain text with no source, this space is not shown.
2The words, in the middle. Unchecked machine words keep their amber dot. To select some words, tap the first and then the last; no press-and-drag is needed on a phone.
3Three answers, the same everywhere. Right: every word is just so; it is checked and the next one comes up. Fix: type the correct words, either the selected words or the whole line. Not sure: the words are kept as [words?], the model does not learn from them, and you can ask about them on the talk page.
4Fixes gather in a tray and go as ONE Suggestion for each place (each recording, scan or page), so the right keepers approve each one. No more one Suggestion per word and no Combine step afterwards. The tray survives leaving the page (it is kept in this browser), and Send shows every change before it goes.
Two ways in, one sheet Check (from “To check”, a project, or the machine dot): a queue that brings up one line or paragraph at a time and moves on by itself. Fix (from any page, with the Help level on): tap what is wrong, and the same sheet opens on just that.

Today → new, with nothing lost

Where Today New
Editing a page
/edit/:id		 A separate edit page. You click a segment and it opens in place, with bold, italic and underline, and you write a line about the change. Each fix is its own Suggestion. The name and date are in another form. No separate page. With the Help level on, you tap a paragraph on the reading page to open the Fix sheet. Bold, italic and underline are in its toolbar. A choice of what was fixed (spelling, missing words, punctuation, other) replaces writing a line. Fixing the name or date is in the ⋯ menu, in the same sheet. Adding a paragraph after this one, and writing a page's first words, are kept. Board 4.
Checking a scan
/text/:scan		 The page and its lines side by side, which is too small on a phone. You tap a line to fix it, “This page is right” raises a page a level, and a strip of coloured squares shows the pages. One line at a time on a phone: the scan cut to the line sits above the words the machine read, with the three answers below. When every line on a page is marked Right, the page goes up a level. “The whole page is right” is in ⋯. The page strip becomes a slim progress bar, and tapping it opens the strip. Uploading your own OCR and the keepers' pick stay in ⋯ › Text layers. Board 5.
The transcript page
Transcripts + player		 The paragraph being heard is marked, and within it the word. A tap plays from a paragraph. Fixing happens elsewhere, in Review machine text. The player is the site's small bar. The word being said is a solid red mark. Words still to come are grey, and the paragraph being heard sits on a lifted card. The page follows the voice, and once you scroll, a “Back to the word being said” button appears. Tapping a word plays from it; at the Help level it also opens Fix, Hear again and Not sure, and can pause on the word. The player has a 68px play button, back 5 seconds, next paragraph, speed, and a map of the paragraphs coloured checked or machine. Boards 6a (you can try it) and 6b.
Checking a transcript
Review machine text		 Four paragraph buttons: All exact, Edit paragraph (with “I checked all of it”), Unclear and History. You select words by pressing and dragging, and a help box explains seven rules. The three answers, with no help box needed. You select words by tapping the first and the last. Fixing some words leaves the paragraph unchecked, just as the server does now (complete: false); Right then checks it. Not sure takes the place of Unclear. History moves to ⋯. The recording still pauses while you type and still comes back to where you stopped. When the last paragraph is done, a question appears: “Did the highlight follow the words?”, which confirms the sync. Board 6, which you can try.
Sending Each segment or word is sent the moment you save it, so one session makes many Suggestions. The Fixes tray holds everything from the session. Send shows each change as a small diff with a note already filled in, and makes one Suggestion. If you may approve your own, the button reads “Save” and the change goes live at once. Board 7.
Reviewing fixes
TranscriptFixes		 Keep or Remove on each fix, then Apply; Combine joins one person's many Suggestions into one. The same tray seen by the person approving: Keep or Remove on each change, with a tap to hear or see it, then Approve. Combine is still there for older Suggestions, but new ones no longer need it. Board 8.

Phone first, by rule

The answers sit at the bottom, where the thumb reaches. The source stays fixed at the top. When the keyboard opens, the sheet rises with it and the words being fixed stay in view. Every target is at least 44px, and no tool appears only on hover.

Larger screens add, never change

On a tablet or desktop the source moves beside the words. Keys: Enter Right, F Fix, U Not sure, J/K next and back, Space play or pause, R hear again. Board 9.

Always labelled, always undoable

The machine dot goes only when a person answers Right or fixes the whole line. Every answer can be undone from the toast or the tray, and every version stays in History.

Plain words only

Right, Fix, Not sure, Your fixes, Send, Approve: נכון · תיקון · לא בטוח · התיקונים שלך · שליחה · אישור. Segment, commit and OCR layer appear only in help.

Building it, in order

A · The partsCheckSheet (source, words, answers), WordPicker (tap the first and last word), FixesTray (kept in this browser), and an answer toast with Undo.
B · TranscriptsThe transcript page and its player come first (6a), then TranscriptEditor moves onto the parts, because these get the most use. The server is unchanged except for one Suggestion holding many fixes.
C · Scans/text/:scan shows one line at a time on a phone, using the line boxes the OCR already keeps.
D · PagesFix opens in the reader, and /edit/:id redirects there with the Fix sheet open, so old links keep working.
E · ApprovingThe review page uses the tray, with Keep or Remove on each change and one Approve.

- New site: Tanya and Shulchan Aruch HaRav read as books of chapters and simanim. Tanya goes by part, then chapter, with a row of the part's chapters under the title. The opening words of each paragraph are bold as printed, the nikud is kept, and the daily-shiur markers stay hidden. Where a chapter has a translation it reads in Hebrew, English, or both side by side, with the translation's notes under it. Shulchan Aruch HaRav goes by section, then siman: each seif is numbered in the margin and opens by its own link (`#s-3`), words in parentheses are a little quieter, and the preface and siman name that the print sets over the first seif stand apart. Chips show the siman's count of seifim and link to its other edition (Mahadura Kama or Tinyana). (#255)

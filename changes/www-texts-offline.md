- New site: a sicha's words are kept at their own address, `/_/words/<id>/<key>` (the key is the hash of the words), cached for good by the browser, the edge and the site's service worker. A click to a sicha shows the page at once and draws the words in after; a sicha read before opens again with none of its words over the network. The service worker also keeps the last copy of every page read, so pages and sichos already opened work offline, and a page never opened says so in Hebrew (#230).

- Built-in schema 19 (RFC 0004, step 2): a sefer may say which view draws it (`view`: sichos, letters, hisvaaduyos, index, daybook, chapters, verses, diary, siddur), and a unit may carry the facts its view shows under its title: `kind` (a hosafa is its own kind), `heading` (a maamar's ד"ה), `number`, `to`, `place`, `occasion`, `parsha`, `muga`, and `labelOrigin` while a machine's guess is unchecked. A page's words take the segment kind `stage` for what was done at a farbrengen (ניגנו). All optional; a catalog takes the new schemas at start, with no migration.

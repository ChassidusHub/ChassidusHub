- A Sichos Kodesh farbrengen is read as one page on the new site: all its
  sichos one after another, each under its own name ("שיחה א", "שיחה ב"…),
  their notes at the end, and the farbrengens before and after at the foot of
  the page. A single sicha's address opens the farbrengen at that sicha. Each
  sicha stays its own item, so a fix goes to the sicha it is in.

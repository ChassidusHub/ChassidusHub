- New site: Hayom Yom reads as the printed book, a day to a page, as the old site set it: the weekday, the day and the year in one row, the day's notices, its shiurim (Chumash, Tehillim, Tanya) as printed, each a link to its words on the site, then the sayings; the letter before 19 Kislev and the closing blessing as in the print, marked as read by a machine until a person checks them. A strip of the month's days sits under the title, and the day before and after at the foot; no "today", nothing to tick. (#254)
